### Best Warriors X
Best Warriors X or, for the most intimate, 
BWX, is the most important warrior tournament on the planet. 
Participants from all over the world compete against each other in regional competitions.
some of them life and death, which will give access to the continental tournament.
Only one winner from each continent gets a place in this incredible tournament.
We are in the third edition of this event and we already have the largest audience on the planet.
Now four of the greatest warriors will battle to decide who is the strongest warrior of the year.

**This game require 4 joysticks to be played**
